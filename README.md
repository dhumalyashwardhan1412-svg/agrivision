## 🌾 About AgriVision

**AgriVision** is a smart agriculture platform developed to provide farmers with useful digital tools in one place.

The system helps farmers manage their farming profile, view agricultural market information, use AI-powered assistance, explore farming products, and directly participate in an agricultural marketplace.

The main goal of AgriVision is to reduce the technology gap in agriculture and help farmers:

- 🌱 Grow smarter
- 📊 Understand market prices
- 🛒 Buy agricultural products
- 🤝 Sell products directly
- 🤖 Get AI-based assistance
- 📍 Receive location-based information
- 💰 Improve farming income

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 👨‍🌾 Farmer Registration & Profile

Farmers can create their accounts and provide important farming information such as:

- Name
- State
- District
- Farm location
- Total land / acreage
- Farming details

This information is used to personalize the AgriVision experience.

---

### 📊 Market Intelligence

Farmers can explore agricultural market information including:

- Commodity prices
- Market trends
- State-wise prices
- District-wise information
- Price comparisons
- Historical market information

---

### 🛒 Agricultural Marketplace

AgriVision provides a digital marketplace where agricultural products can be explored and managed.

Features include:

- Product listings
- Seller information
- Product prices
- Buying and selling
- Order management
- Direct farmer marketplace

---

### 🤖 AI Farming Assistant

The integrated AI assistant can help farmers with agricultural questions such as:

- Crop-related queries
- Farming suggestions
- Fertilizer information
- General agricultural guidance
- Market-related questions

---

### 🌍 Location-Based Experience

AgriVision uses the farmer's registered location to provide more relevant information.

For example:

**State:** Maharashtra  
**District:** Pune

The system can use this information to personalize market and farming information.

---

### 🛍️ Agriculture Shop

Farmers can explore agricultural products such as:

- Seeds
- Fertilizers
- Farming equipment
- Agricultural tools
- Other farming-related products

---

### 📦 Order Management

Users can manage their marketplace and shop orders through the AgriVision platform.
